##### KAFKA

**(1Q) What is Apache Kafka?**

Apache Kafka is a tool that helps different parts of an application share information by sending messages quickly and efficiently. It's like a post office of data , ensuring the messages are sent,received and processed in real time, even if there's a lot of data. It is used by lot of applications that need to handle data immediately, like tracjing clicks on website or processing online orders.
